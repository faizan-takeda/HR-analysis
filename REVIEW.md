## Review method and release boundary
Design, content and product review perspectives were applied by the same assistant, not independently running agents. Chapter-level browser checks, model assertions and scene-graph assertions are in `tests/` and `QA_RESULTS.json`. WebGL initialization remains blocked in this environment, including software ANGLE attempts. Three.js actor positions, network draw counts and rework sample states were checked directly without rendering; fallback visuals were rendered and inspected in Chromium. Live GPU animation quality, actual corporate device performance, Safari and deployment were not verified. No independent 10/10 or stakeholder approval is claimed.
