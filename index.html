Problem

02

03

Proposed mechanism

02 / 11 Future capabilities

Tools change. Judgment must endure.

Build transferable capability, not just familiarity with today’s software.

01

Problem

Training and tool adoption do not demonstrate independent, safe performance.

02

Scenario · impact

A new AI tool accelerates analysis, but an unverified output can create scientific or operational risk.

03

Proposed mechanism

Define role-specific critical tasks; assess durable reasoning and validate emerging skills through supervised work.

04

Transferable learning

WEF expects 39% of core skills to change by 2030; NIST NICE connects cyber skills to tasks.

R&D: interpret experimental evidence · Security: evaluate threats · Commercial: assess market evidence.

Decision

Use critical-task profiles in the selected missions.

Success test

Measure successful independent task completion, quality and time to competence.

08 / 11 Coordination and reuse

03

Proposed mechanism

04

Transferable learning

03

Proposed mechanism

04

Transferable learning

Refine — the outcome is not yet proven. Improve the test before expanding.

Decision

Use common evidence gates in existing portfolio processes.

Success test

Scale only when the agreed outcome improves and quality, fairness and feasibility hold.

11 / 11 Enterprise decision

Every mission should make Takeda stronger.

A connected people strategy: better talent, better delivery, lasting capability.

01

Attract

Hire for judgment. Use structured evidence to select people who can solve changing problems.

02

Develop & deploy

Develop and deploy existing expertise. Match qualified people to the work that matters.

03

Connect & own

Connect work and retain ownership. Discover before building; agree acceptance before outsourcing.

04

Validate & reinvest

Prove value and reinvest. Capture outcomes, validated skills and reusable knowledge for the next mission.

Discover→Develop / hire→Deliver→Validate & reuse

The next mission starts stronger: more expertise, less reinvention, clearer ownership.

Decision

Authorize two bounded missions using existing systems and a common evidence standard.

Success test

Track delivery, quality, capability growth and retained knowledge. Scale only what works.
