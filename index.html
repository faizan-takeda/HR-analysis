05 / Evidence-based selection

Make hiring more rigorous, not longer.

A consistent evidence contract across job families, with proportionate role-specific assessments.

01

Define

Job analysis, must-have competencies, success measures and scoring anchors.

02

Demonstrate

Short, realistic work sample; change a constraint to test learning.

03

Calibrate

Structured interview, independent scores, trained cross-team reviewer.

04

Validate

Human decision; compare evidence to six- and twelve-month outcomes.

Test the right thing

R&D: critique conflicting evidence · Cyber: prioritize a simulated incident · Commercial: defend a market-access choice · Manufacturing: diagnose a process deviation.

Protect the candidate

Accessible alternatives; no unpaid production work; declared AI rules; privacy, local law and fairness review. No opaque automated rejection.

Example rubric: 1–5 anchored evidenceProblem solvingRole depthAdaptationJudgment
